# Fine-Tuning as a Service on the AmSC — Design

![](ftaas-design.png)

::: notes
Genesis has produced 12+ scientific foundation models, and every downstream fusion
task needs its own fine-tune. Fine-tuning is not a training job: it needs simulation,
training and inference running concurrently, steered while it runs, with a workload
shape that is not known at submission time.

FTaaS is that campaign submitted as a request. The client names a base model, data
sources, an evaluation criterion and a stopping rule. IRI carries identity, facility
status and scheduling. RHAPSODY-Service brokers the request out to a private RHAPSODY
instance per site, where one allocation holds simulation, training and inference side
by side. MLflow / ClearML carry experiments and the model catalog.

Same substrate as ROSE-aaS in Aymen's slides, and as DTaaS — one platform, three
science services.
:::

# FTaaS — where we stand

**Runs on Perlmutter today**

- Fine-tuning campaign (MATEY) driven through the IRI / SFAPI path.
- Simulation and training side by side in one allocation — concurrent Dragon and Flux partitions, validated against Flux 0.79.
- Multiple inference services per allocation, explicit node placement, typed model / hardware / batching configuration.

**RHAPSODY at scale — the substrate this rests on** (arXiv 2512.20795)

- ~11k tasks/s sustained peak, near-linear weak scaling, a distinct task on every node.
- ~50,000 active agents: sustained overlap of AI decision rate and HPC task realization.
- 100–300 µs per-task overhead, 90–95% GPU utilization; Frontier, Perlmutter, Anvil.

**Open work** — the submission surface that turns the recipe into a service; concurrent fine-tunes sharing one allocation; catalog, lineage and promotion to downstream tasks.

::: notes
Honest split: the runtime numbers are measured and published; the Perlmutter path is a
working recipe driven by a script. What is missing is the submission surface — a user
asking the platform for a fine-tune rather than driving one. Transport, placement and
MLOps integration are already deployed across OLCF, ALCF and NERSC.
:::
