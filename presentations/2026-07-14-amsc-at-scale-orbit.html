From your laptop to Perlmutter GPUs —

HPC services scale — changing them is what's costly

  • Facility-deployed services scale well — that's not the problem
    • the problem is change: adapting to per-user demands is costly and slow
    • user-level configurability — which AI models, service parameters, versions — doesn't fit the deployment cycle
  • Meanwhile users already embed AI in their own small-scale, local workflows
    • a PyTorch instance here, a vLLM server there, custom(ized) models
    • fully under their control — but not at facility scale
  • And deploying their own services at the facility? "a no-go for end users"
  • What we are aiming for: user-instantiated, user-configured AI backends — at scale, with nothing but an allocation

The stack — what does what

  your science workflow — or a campaign of them
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ROSE        learn      simulation + surrogate training + model
                         selection loops (active / reinforcement learning)
  AsyncFlow   compose    async workflows, task dependencies, campaigns
  RHAPSODY    execute    ComputeTask + AITask in one allocation;
                         embedded AI backends (vLLM serving, …)
  ORBIT       connect    HPC resources ⇄ one workflow / campaign
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Perlmutter / NERSC  ·  Odo, Frontier / OLCF  ·  …
  • Embedded AI services are RHAPSODY's and ROSE's job — models served inside the allocation, learning loops as a service
  • ORBIT connects — multiple HPC resources become one workflow, or one campaign of workflows
  • …and keeps the layers configurable on the fly: which RHAPSODY / ROSE services run where, with which models and parameters

ORBIT: scalable, embedded AI backends in user land

  laptop / workstation ····· HTTPS (outbound) ·····▶ ╔══════════╗
    └─ driver script                                 ║  BROKER  ║   the only public component
                                                     ╚══════════╝
                                                          ▲
                                  outbound WebSocket ·····┘
  HPC compute nodes ······································┘
    └─ endpoint  (a normal batch job, spawned on demand via IRI / SFAPI / Slurm / …)
         └─ RHAPSODY ▸ your tasks — GPU + CPU, pinned per device
  • Everything connects out to the broker — firewall-friendly by construction
  • The compute-side service lives inside the user's allocation: spawned on demand, torn down when done
  • No root, no facility deployment, no inbound port — scales with users, not with ops
Formerly bridge/edge — renamed broker/endpoint mid-2026; older demo material shows the legacy names.

AI-workflow integration

  • Heterogeneous tasks in one allocation — train, infer, simulate run concurrently, GPU + CPU side by side, pinned per device (RHAPSODY on Dragon)
  • Resource slicing — nodes shared between services and tasks (horizontal) or partitioned per service (vertical): one config switch
  • ROSE-aaS — active-learning & model-service orchestration as a service (the outer ML loop SURGE et al. don't have)
  • Experiment tracking — MLflow / ClearML wired into the drivers, reporting to the AmSC MLflow server
  • Bring your own workload — in the fine-tuning recipe only 3 things are MATEY-specific: application paths, env wrapper script, task command line — swap those, keep the machinery
